# Social icon provenance

The seven brand SVG paths in public/social-icons are from [Simple Icons 16.0.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/16.0.0/icons). Only an explicit black fill was added to prevent dark color-scheme inheritance from making monochrome icons invisible on their white tile. Paths and aspect ratios remain unchanged. website.svg is an original generic globe under this repository's license.

Simple Icons publishes under CC0 1.0 Universal. Its complete LICENSE and DISCLAIMER are retained here. CC0 does not grant trademark rights; upstream explicitly cautions that individual marks may have additional terms. The selected v16 metadata lists no separate icon licenses. These are optional, destination-identification marks beside broadcaster-entered text, not application branding, an endorsement claim or platform integration. Use appropriate clearspace, avoid distortion, and follow each owner's current guidelines for your broadcast.

Upstream metadata and original guideline links are retained so broadcasters can check their particular use.
